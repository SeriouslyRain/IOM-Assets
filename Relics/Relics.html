How to Read This Calculator

Enter your total relics, then choose one mode. The calculator updates instantly and uses one full reroll = 200 gems.

Option 1 (solves for gem cost): Enter bought divine relics and a target number of divine rolls. Bought divines are guaranteed and are added to the reroll outcome when calculating target chance, expected rerolls, and expected cost.

Option 2 (solves for target): Enter gem budget and desired success chance. The tool finds the highest integer target that meets that cumulative success chance across your budgeted rerolls. Bought divine relics are ignored in this mode.

First column: Mode-specific planning stats. In Option 2, "total probability of hitting target" is cumulative across budgeted rerolls, while "probability for each roll" is the single-reroll chance for that solved target.

Second column: Distribution reference (mean and +1/+2/+3 standard-deviation markers). This describes the outcome spread used by the probability bands.

Third column: Deviation-band probabilities. In Option 1 these are for one reroll; in Option 2 they are for total outcomes across all budgeted rerolls.
