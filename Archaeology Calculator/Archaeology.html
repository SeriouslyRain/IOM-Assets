Push optimizer

Uses upgrades and settings from Your build. Screens many stat layouts, refines the best, then confirms at full MC trials. Optimizer runs MC in parallel via js/mc-worker.js (uses several CPU cores).

Results

Preview your current stats, or optimize to see results for recommended stats.

Push sim: per-slot spawns, spawn-order targeting, rolled crits (incl. super/ultra), block cards, Enrage / Flurry / Quake, partial floors. No block speed mods.

XP optimizer

Uses the same build and MC trials as push. Screens many stat layouts with quick MC, refines the best, then confirms at full trials (good when budget ≪ sum of caps). MC screening uses parallel workers like push.

XP results

Preview your current stats, or optimize to see results for recommended stats.

XP sim: rolled crits, block cards (tier + dirt), deterministic block mods, flurry speed + stamina, enrage/quake cleave rolls, partial floors. Results show E[XP/hr] (mean of per-run rates) and ratio÷means (sum XP ÷ sum time — cross-tool compare). Crosshairs / one-hit not simulated.

Fragment optimizer
